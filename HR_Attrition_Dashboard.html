Findings use all 1,464 employees; the filters on other pages don't change this page.

16.1%

Attrition rate — 235 of 1,464 employees

Roughly one in six. Administration (87) and Sales (52) account for 59% of all leavers.

3×

Overtime triples attrition

30.6% of staff on overtime left vs 10.3% without. Single employees on overtime: 42.2%. Under-26s on overtime: 64% (25 of 39).

34%

Early-tenure exodus

34.3% of employees with under two years' tenure left — 73 people, 31% of all leavers. Attrition falls to 8.1% after ten years.

60%

Of leavers are entry level

Job level 1 runs at 26.2% attrition (141 leavers). Entry-level staff on overtime: 52.9% left.

39.5%

Sales Representatives are the hottest role

Followed by Laboratory Technicians (23.7%, 61 leavers — the largest single group). Managers 4.9%, Research Directors 2.5%.

24% → 9%

Even one level of stock options retains

No options: 24.3% attrition. Option level 1: 9.4%. 629 employees (43%) hold none.

25%

Frequent travellers leave at 3× non-travellers

24.7% vs 8.0%. Rare travel sits between at 15.0%.

34% vs 9%

Disengagement is an early warning

Lowest job-involvement score: 34.1% left vs 9.1% at the top score. Poor work-life balance (score 1): 31.2%.

₹51k vs ₹49k

Pay isn't explaining exits — check the pay data

Leavers' median income (₹51.0k/month) is in line with stayers (₹49.3k). Income doesn't rise with job level or experience in this file, so pay figures should be verified with the payroll system before pay decisions.

Recommended actions

  1. Cap and review overtime for entry-level and under-26 staffHighest-risk group in the data (53–64% attrition). Track overtime hours monthly by manager.
  2. Rebuild the first two yearsStructured onboarding, 90-day and 12-month stay interviews, and a buddy programme — targeting the 34% early-tenure loss.
  3. Extend stock option level 1 to entry levelMoving from no options to level 1 coincides with attrition falling from 24% to 9%. Cost this against replacement cost for 538 level-1 roles.
  4. Role deep-dives: Sales Representative and Laboratory TechnicianReview workload, travel, career paths and manager quality; set a target to bring both under 20%.
  5. Cut frequent travel where possibleRotation or regional coverage for the 271 frequent travellers company-wide (24.7% attrition vs 8.0% for non-travellers).
  6. Quarterly engagement pulse with alertsFlag low involvement, work-life balance and environment scores for manager follow-up before resignation.

These are correlations from one snapshot, not proven causes; pilot changes and measure attrition in the treated groups.

    Caveats for decision-makers

    • Monthly income looks unreliable. It doesn't rise with job level (level 1 median ₹51.9k vs level 5 ₹49.3k) or experience (correlation −0.02). Treat pay findings with caution.
    • 14 employee IDs appeared twice with different income values. The most complete record was kept; the source of conflict should be checked in HRIS.
    • Snapshot data. No exit dates, so this shows who left, not when or trends over time.
    • Finance (24) and Human Resources (39) are small departments — their rates move a lot with a few exits.
